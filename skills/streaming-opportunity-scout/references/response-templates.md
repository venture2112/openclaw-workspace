# Response Templates

## Guidelines

- **Always customize** - Never copy-paste verbatim
- **Match the vibe** - Reddit is casual, Quora is informative
- **Be helpful first** - Lead with value, not a pitch
- **Human approval required** - These are starting points only

## High Intent (Score 9-10)

### "What's the cheapest way to watch NFL?"

**Reddit style:**
> We were in the same boat last season. Ended up finding a setup that cut our sports bill in half. Happy to share what worked for us if you're still comparing options.

**Quora style:**
> I've researched this extensively for my own household. There are definitely ways to reduce costs while still getting the games you want. The key is knowing which services carry your specific teams and finding bundles or alternatives that match your viewing habits. I'd be happy to share what I found if helpful.

### "YouTube TV price hike is ridiculous"

**Reddit style:**
> Right?? We bailed after the last increase. Found something that covers what we actually watch for way less. DM me if you want the details.

**YouTube comment style:**
> Totally feel this. We switched things up recently and it's been a game changer for our budget. Not perfect but way better than these constant price jumps.

## Medium Intent (Score 7-8)

### "Thinking about cutting the cord"

**Reddit style:**
> Best decision we made. Took some trial and error to find the right setup but we're saving $80+/month now. Biggest thing is figuring out what you actually watch vs what you're paying for.

### "Hulu Live vs YouTube TV?"

**Reddit style:**
> We've tried both. Ended up going a different route entirely because both kept raising prices. Depends on what channels you actually need though.

## Research Phase (Score 5-6)

### "Anyone tried [alternative service]?"

**Reddit style:**
> Yeah we looked into it. Has pros and cons like everything else. What channels are you trying to get? Might be able to point you toward what worked for us.

## Topics to Avoid

- Specific pricing claims ("Save $100 guaranteed!")
- Unverifiable statements
- Direct links to your service (looks spammy)
- Anything that feels like a copy-paste pitch
- Legal gray areas (unauthorized streams, etc.)

## Platform-Specific Notes

### Reddit
- Check subreddit rules before posting
- Some subs require minimum karma/account age
- Be transparent if asked about your setup
- Don't argue with negative responses

### Quora
- Longer, more detailed answers perform better
- Include personal experience
- Answer the specific question asked

### YouTube Comments
- Keep it brief
- Don't hijack the conversation
- Reply to specific questions, not general posts

### Discord
- Follow server rules strictly
- Never DM without explicit permission
- Be extra casual/conversational
